Getting a good count

  • Prop your iPhone up 2 to 3 meters away, in portrait.
  • Use bright light, with your whole body and the ball in view.
  • Face the camera.
  • Kick the ball above your knees: lower touches don't count.

Camera access

If the camera screen says Camera Access Needed, open the Settings app, find Footy, and turn on Camera. Video stays on your iPhone.

Footy Plus

Footy Plus is an optional subscription with unlimited challenge tries, your full run history and progress charts. It renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel it in Settings, under your Apple Account, Subscriptions. To restore a purchase, open Footy Plus in the app and tap Restore Subscription. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Leaderboards

Leaderboards and friends' scores use Apple's Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app to see them.
