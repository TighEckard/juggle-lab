Footy Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Footy is an iPhone app that counts your soccer juggles with the camera. We built it so your data stays on your iPhone unless you sign in to use teams and leaderboards. This policy explains what the app uses and why.

Camera

Footy uses the camera to see you and the ball and count your touches. This is processed on your iPhone by tracking code built into the app. Video is never uploaded to us or anyone else. Footy doesn't use the microphone. A short clip of your run (recorded with Apple's screen recording, which asks you first) is kept on your iPhone so you can watch or share it, and it's only shared if you choose to share it.

Your stats

Your runs, training sessions, XP, streaks and other stats are stored on your iPhone. Without an account, nothing leaves your iPhone.

Accounts, teams and leaderboards

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Apple, Footy creates an account for you with Google Firebase, which we use to run teams and the Footy leaderboards. We then store:

  • Your account ID, and the name Apple shares when you sign in (you can choose not to share it, and then you appear as "Player").
  • Your summary stats: best scores in each challenge, XP, training time, juggles counted, longest juggle time, drills done and streaks. Not your individual runs, and never video.
  • The teams you create or join, including team names and team codes.
  • Reports you send about another player's name.

Your name and summary stats are shown to other signed-in Footy players on the leaderboards, and to your teammates. We use this data only to run these features. Firebase is operated by Google under the Firebase privacy terms. You can report or block any player from their profile.

Earlier versions of Footy used Apple's Game Center for leaderboards. Game Center scores are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

Footy Plus is a subscription sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. We don't see your payment details. You can manage or cancel it in Settings, under your Apple Account, Subscriptions.

Notifications

If you allow them, reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No data is sent to send them.

Reporting a problem

If you tap Report a Problem, Footy opens an email to us in your Mail app. It can include a short technical log of that run (where the ball and body were seen, frame by frame; no video or photos). Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email, and we use it only to fix counting problems.

Footy on Android

Footy is also available for Android phones. Everything above applies there too, with these differences. The camera is processed on the phone and video is never uploaded. Your stats stay on the phone. The Android app doesn't record clips of your runs. Reminders are scheduled on the phone, and Report a Problem opens an email in your email app that can include your app version, phone model and Android version, an optional note, and the same short technical log.

Google Play Games

On Android, if you sign in to Google Play Games, Footy sends your Play Games player ID and your challenge scores and weekly XP to Google Play Games so they can appear on the Footy leaderboards. This is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. Signing in is optional, and you can manage Play Games in your Google account.

Google Play purchases

On Android, Footy Plus is sold and managed by Google through Google Play Billing. We don't see your payment details. To manage or cancel it, open Google Play, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

Deleting your data on Android

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Settings removes its data from your phone. Play Games data is managed through your Google account. To ask us to delete an email you sent us, write to the address under Changes and questions.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics, and nothing collected beyond what's listed above.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Age

Footy is meant for players aged 13 and up.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. If you signed in, you can delete your account in the app (Profile, Settings, Delete Account, or the account menu on the Team tab). This deletes your account, your scores on our servers and your team memberships right away. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions: email tigheckard@gmail.com or see Footy Support.
