Footy Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Footy is an iPhone app that counts your soccer juggles with the camera. We built it so your data stays on your iPhone. This policy explains what the app uses and why.

Camera and microphone

Footy uses the camera to see you and the ball and count your touches. The microphone is optional and listens for the sound of the ball. All of this is processed on your iPhone. Video and audio are never uploaded to us or anyone else. A short clip of your run is kept on your iPhone so you can watch or share it, and it's only shared if you choose to share it.

Your stats

Your runs, training sessions, XP, streaks and other stats are stored on your iPhone. We don't have servers that collect them, and we don't have accounts.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, Footy posts your daily challenge scores to the Footy leaderboard and shows your Game Center friends. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can turn Game Center off in the Settings app.

Purchases

Footy Plus is a subscription sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. We don't see your payment details. You can manage or cancel it in Settings, under your Apple Account, Subscriptions.

Notifications

If you allow them, reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No data is sent to send them.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or data collection by us.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Age

Footy is meant for players aged 13 and up.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Game Center scores can be managed in Game Center.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. For questions, use the support link on Footy's App Store page.
